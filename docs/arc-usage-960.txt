Arc's gas token is USDC carried with 18 decimals at the EVM level, so gasUsed * tx.gasprice is a dollar amount directly, with no price feed in the expression. That is not true on any other chain: where the gas token floats, a contract needs an oracle to know what it just cost, and inherits its staleness and failure modes. Here the unit of gas is the unit of account.

ArcMeter is the smallest useful thing built on that. A permissionless, append-only registry of what EVM operations actually cost on Arc, measured from inside transactions. Anyone can refresh a sample; no owner, no admin, nothing editable once written.

It also exercises the chain's dual USDC representation: costs are published in native 18-decimal units and in the 6-decimal form the predeploy at 0x3600...0000 reports, related by exactly 1e12.
