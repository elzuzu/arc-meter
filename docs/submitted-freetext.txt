ArcMeter is a permissionless, append-only registry of what specific EVM operations actually cost on Arc, measured from inside transactions rather than copied out of a table. Because Arc's gas token is USDC carried with 18 decimals, every sample is published as both a gas count and a dollar amount, with no price feed anywhere in the calculation.
